# Majekodunmi Immanuel — Portfolio

## Product

Personal portfolio for **Majekodunmi Immanuel**, Software Engineer based in Lagos, Nigeria.

**Surface type:** Brand (marketing / portfolio landing)

**Purpose:** Persuade recruiters and collaborators with editorial clarity — selected live product work, experience, and contact — not a dashboard or blog.

**Audience:** Hiring managers, design-minded engineers, founders evaluating frontend craft.

## Brand

- **Name (hero signal):** Majekodunmi Immanuel
- **Voice:** Precise, calm, craft-forward. Short sentences. No hype adjectives.
- **Anti-references:** SaaS template portfolios, purple gradients, Inter/Roboto, nested cards, dark neon glow, pill badge clusters, cream+terracotta “AI editorial” clichés.

## Content pillars

1. Hero brand + one supporting line + Work/Contact CTAs
2. Selected Work — Abode, Abode Academy, TicksOTR, City Spaces (live URLs)
3. About, History, Skills, Contact

## Success

Feels as sleek as [annaparellada.com](https://www.annaparellada.com/): typography-led, sparse chrome, intentional motion, one job per section.
